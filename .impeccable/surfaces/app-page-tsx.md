## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one toy box you open. It refuses the meme-coin default (mascot hero, pie-chart tokenomics, roadmap cards): the mechanism is told by bricks moving through Bricktown, from heap to furnace.
OWN-WORLD: Cobalt printed box exterior, kraft cardboard interior, furnace-night for the burn. Isometric studded bricks in tomato, sun, mint, sky, snow on a green baseplate. Dela Gothic One display, Bricolage Grotesque body, JetBrains Mono for addresses and amounts. Buttons are bricks with studs that press down.
STORY: Visitor sees the box front (what it is, buy, CA), lifts the lid (four bags), walks the four bags (supply, fees, buyback, burn) while the scene changes from day to night, sees the furnace log, reads the box side panel (contents), follows the building instructions, ends at the box back (socials, independence note).
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5/11: brick-built BRICK wordmark, H1 "Every trade feeds the furnace.", 18-word sub, brick Buy button + CA chip. Right 6/11: tilted box-art window with the isometric Bricktown scene, 18+ age tab top-left, tomato 1,000,000,000 pcs sticker bottom-right. Fixed cobalt nav above.
FORM: Toy box art (candidate 3 of 7 on the grounded list: factory furnace, bedroom floor, box art, instruction booklet, diorama, brickfilm, parts catalogue). seed: none (unavailable); roll key 4eaff510. Raises: wordmark assembles from falling bricks (alphabet-storm), counters move on one shared downbeat (VU meter), skyline parallax depth layers (cracktro). Signature interaction: scroll lifts the lid; scroll-scrubbed four-bag story with snap to chapters.
